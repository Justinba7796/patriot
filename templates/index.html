Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward Answers.
No Guesswork.

What is a mobile mechanic?

A mobile mechanic performs eligible automotive diagnostics, maintenance and repairs at your location instead of requiring a visit to a traditional shop.

Can you repair my car at home or work?

Mobile repairs may be performed at a suitable home or workplace location, depending on the repair, available space and property permission.

What if my car won't start?

Starting problems can involve your battery, starter, charging system or electrical components. Diagnostics help determine the actual cause.

How much does mobile auto repair cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, requested service, required parts and diagnostic work. Contact Patriot to discuss your specific repair needs.

Are all repairs possible on-site?

Some repairs require specialized shop equipment or facilities. Contact Patriot to determine whether your vehicle can be serviced at your location.
